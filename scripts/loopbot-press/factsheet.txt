Short description (50 words)
----------------------------
LoopBot is a coding puzzle game. A rogue AI has taken over the city's tallest
skyscraper, and you program a small maintenance robot to climb all 50 floors and
take it back. Snap together blocks, press Run, and learn loops, functions and
logic one floor at a time.

Long description
----------------
VANTA, a rogue salvage AI, has taken over the Meridian Spire, the tallest
skyscraper in the city, floor by floor. You're LoopBot, a small maintenance robot
with a big job. With PATCH talking you through it over the radio, you climb all 50
floors, one puzzle at a time, to take the tower back.

You don't steer LoopBot. You program it. Plan a route with blocks like forward,
turn and jump, press Run, and see if your plan works. When it doesn't, LoopBot
shows you exactly where it went wrong, so you can fix it and try again.

Every floor fits on one screen and teaches a single idea, from simple sequences in
the Lobby to typing real code on the Rooftop. A Daily puzzle, achievements and
unlockable looks keep players coming back. LoopBot is free, has no ads, works
offline and is fully translated into French. It's built by one developer with
React, Phaser and Capacitor, and every floor is checked by a solver so its par is
always fair.

Features
--------
- 50 free floors in five zones
- Blocks plus a real-code view, typed code on the top floors
- Daily puzzle with streaks
- Achievements and the Locker
- English and French
- Offline, no account, no ads
- Accessibility options
